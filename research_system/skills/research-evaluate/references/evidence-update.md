# Evidence and belief updates

Inspect raw evidence and its provenance. Compare the observation with the predictions and assumptions that existed for that run or proof attempt. Distinguish supports, contradicts, inconclusive and failed_run. Check competing explanations, code/measurement errors and the limits of generalization.

Record whether to retain, revise, reject or defer a hypothesis, why, and the next discriminating check. An inconclusive result is a valid endpoint when evidence or resources are insufficient; do not force convergence or a fixed number of iterations.

Use subject references for historical objects being observed/reviewed. Use dependency references for premises whose change invalidates a derived conclusion. Keep both supporting and conflicting evidence; a supported/contradicted conclusion with conflicting evidence needs a review explaining the scope and resolution. The validator only checks explicit linkage, not the semantic sufficiency of that explanation.

For proofs, review definitions, assumptions, each obligation and the formalization when applicable. For empirical claims, inspect design, measurement, uncertainty and raw outputs. Novelty, usefulness and scientific support are distinct assessments. LLM review is an attestation to be checked, not ground truth.
