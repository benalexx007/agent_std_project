# research-evaluate

Select the study rubric. Inspect original artifacts and counterevidence; independently reproduce critical checks when authorized. Report specific findings with location, evidence, impact and checks actually performed. Assign supported, contradicted or inconclusive per claim and scope. Separate transport/schema/hash checks from scientific validity. If evidence is insufficient, describe the discriminating check rather than inventing a verdict or repeating an unbounded critique loop.

Read [evidence updates](references/evidence-update.md) for hypothesis testing, competing explanations and proof evidence levels. For mathematics, use the task's mathematical guidance or inspect definitions/assumptions/obligations directly. Recheck formalization as well as verifier success. Review historical subjects against their pinned revision; do not silently apply their verdict to a changed statement.

For a planning result, inspect whether planning_review contains genuinely new primary-source checks and research, not just a restatement of analysis or an old audit. A higher-tier model and a valid audit schema do not establish content correctness. Preserve disagreements and unresolved blockers for the human reviewer.

Use the role, study guidance, acceptance criteria and effective configuration supplied with the task. Load only the artifacts needed for the current question. Return a concise result with evidence references, performed checks, limitations and unresolved items using the harness result contract when dispatched by the harness. Keep durable evidence outside chat.

For extension results, distinguish the trusted helper receipt, raw source and scientific interpretation. Check failed bibliography blocks, version identity, symbolic assumptions and the actual unit of analysis; helper completion does not supply a scientific verdict.
