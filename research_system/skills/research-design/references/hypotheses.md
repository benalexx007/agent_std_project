# Hypothesis development

Identify the unresolved question and nearest existing explanations. Propose substantively different hypotheses when useful; the number depends on uncertainty and resources. State assumptions, scope, origin/prior work and a prediction or proof obligation for each. Distinguish a data pattern from a proposed mechanism.

Use research-record kind hypothesis. Record a decision (pursue, revise, reject, pause or retain alternatives) and its reason. Search novelty separately; candidate_within_search_scope needs a dated search scope/basis. Never use novelty as a substitute for validity.

Choose the next check for its ability to distinguish explanations or close an obligation within budget. Retain rejected branches, contrary evidence and unresolved alternatives. A new revision preserves its predecessor; records remain available with read-research --record ID --revision N.
